# Supplemental mechanism and NX12 sources

- METU OpenCourseWare, inverted slider crank configurations and the distinction between force-line and moment transmission: https://ocw.metu.edu.tr/pluginfile.php/3961/mod_resource/content/12/ch7/7-3.htm . The source's figure-dependent link labels are not copied into our equations; loop closure and grounded canonical members are recorded explicitly.
- METU four-bar classification and motion: https://ocw.metu.edu.tr/pluginfile.php/3961/mod_resource/content/12/ch7/7-1.htm . Fixed shortest member gives the Grashof double-crank inversion; opposite member gives the Grashof double-rocker inversion.
- Wang Yaqin et al., Mechanical Design Fundamentals, Machinery Industry Press, 2019, ISBN 9787111625452, chapter 3.1 (publisher sample chapter): https://oss0-cdn.changxianggu.com/book/chapter/5_9787111625452.pdf . It distinguishes the slider-crank, guide-bar, oscillating-block and fixed-block inversions. The sample text was fetched, but subsequent diagram fetches timed out; no diagram measurement is claimed.
- Installed Siemens NX12 `NXOpen.xml`: `Motion.DriverOperation.Type.Harmonic`, `AmplitudeExpression`, `FrequencyExpression`, `PhaseAngleExpression`, `HarmonicDisplacementExpression`. These properties predate NX12. Compilation alone is not runtime validation; actual units and native trajectory must be read before accepting a harmonic driver.

The original grounded-chain labels are L0 frame/guide, L1 crank, L2 connecting member, L3 slider, with joints L0-R-L1-R-L2-R-L3-P-L0. The three new inversions ground L1, L2 and L3 respectively. Their unlabeled R-R-R-P graph can be isomorphic; the grounded original member, dimension distribution, physical sleeve/rod/slot construction and actual output motion identify the inversion. Naming or coloring is not evidence.

For internally actuated double-rocker and short oscillating-sleeve output, a geometric angle must not be labeled a measured force-transmission efficiency. Quantities that require an inapplicable force-line assumption are explicitly marked not applicable, with actual position, velocity, mobility and joint closure still checked.

- DMG-Lib / Ilmenau TU external Geneva reference: https://www.dmg-lib.org/dmglib/main/portal.jsp?context_setLangCode=de&id=19260023&mainNaviState=browsen.images.viewer . Consulted for pin engagement and locking-disk topology. The local swept-envelope relief and numerical dimensions are independently synthesized; no NX success is inferred from the diagram.
- Carnegie Mellon mechanism notes, chapter 8: https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~rapidproto/mechanisms/chpt8.html . Ratchet wheel, oscillating driving lever, driving pawl and supplementary reverse-locking pawl. Used as a topology reference; dynamic contact and preload still require local NX validation.
- Installed NX12 API metadata inspected for BodyContactBuilder, BodyContactLms, ScalarTorqueBuilder and ConnectorBuilder. ScalarTorqueType.Joint and ForceValueTypes.Constant are present; metadata availability is not a license or runtime contact test.
